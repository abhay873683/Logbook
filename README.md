# 🌳 TreeFlow AI

> AI-Powered Work Management Platform

TreeFlow AI is a modern AI-powered work management platform designed for companies, teams, and organizations to manage projects, tasks, workflows, documents, reports, and AI-assisted planning in a scalable tree structure.

---

# 🚀 Features

- Multi Company (Multi-Tenant)
- Role Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Department Management
- Project Management
- Tree Structure Management
- Task & Subtask Management
- Team Collaboration
- File & Document Management
- Notifications
- Activity Logs
- Reports & Analytics
- AI Task Generator
- AI Tree Builder
- AI Chat Assistant
- AI Smart Reports
